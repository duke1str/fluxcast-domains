The Framework

Money is only one part of the architecture.

The book connects behavior, ownership, risk, business, investing, and legacy into one practical operating system.

01

Think in ownership

Move beyond earning alone. Build assets, systems, skills, and capital that can compound beyond your direct labor.

02

Protect the downside

Use margin of safety, optionality, and inversion to prevent small mistakes from becoming permanent damage.

03

Create real value

Focus on usefulness, customer outcomes, disciplined execution, and substance rather than appearance or status.

04

Direct the result

Define enough, build with intention, and use wealth as a tool for freedom, family, contribution, and legacy.
